The PACKWELL 18-Tray Commercial Food Dehydrator is an industrial-grade food drying machine engineered for preserving fruits, vegetables, meat jerky, herbs, and seafood. Constructed entirely from food-grade stainless steel, it features 18 drying layers (trays) with individual tray dimensions of 200mm × 285mm, providing a processing capacity of up to 5 kg per drying batch. Equipped with an intuitive digital display control unit and an adjustable temperature range from 0°C to 90°C, it delivers precise thermal and air circulation to retain natural nutrients, flavor, and color. Operating on standard 220V power via a simple plug-in system, it is an essential commercial machine for food processing units, snack businesses, and catering setups.

Why Choose the PACKWELL 18-Tray Commercial Food Dehydrator?

Drying food in direct sunlight is unpredictable, unhygienic, and exposes products to dust, insects, and humidity changes. Standard kitchen ovens lack low-temperature airflow controls, which can easily scorch delicate fruits or herbs instead of dehydrating them.

The PACKWELL 18-Tray Commercial Food Dehydrator provides a clean, temperature-controlled drying environment. With 18 food-grade stainless steel trays, a 5 kg capacity, and precise digital control up to 90°C, it preserves natural flavor, nutrients, and shelf life efficiently. It represents a high-yield, hygienic, and cost-effective solution for small food processing businesses, commercial kitchens, and dry-food startups.
